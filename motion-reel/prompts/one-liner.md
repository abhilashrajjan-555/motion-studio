# One-liner prompts (engine tests, not client work)

A one-liner tests the engine. It never tests the idea, because it doesn't contain one.
Use these to try a new setup, then move on to a brief.

    make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible
    motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé. go all out.

Variants:

    make a 30-second 16:9 showreel as if you were a niche branding studio for startup
    founders. Create every graphic from scratch. One accent color. Every shot is a
    different technique.

    make a dynamic 10-second motion graphics video that introduces [STUDIO NAME].
    Avoid frames and text in the corners, the usual giveaways of AI-made video.

    use your showreel energy, but tell a story: the history of [TOPIC] from [START]
    to today, surprise me with the storyboard. 45 seconds, 16:9.
